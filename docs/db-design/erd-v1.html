How the 17 tables actually connect

Every foreign-key relationship in the DeepSeek-drafted schema (validated against a real Postgres 17 instance and our own Neon project, then renamed for clarity against international naming conventions — see docs/db-design/validation-notes.md), drawn with projects as the structural spine and users as the actor every other table eventually reports to.

Two conventions keep 30 relationships legible instead of tangled: the teal arrows are the structural hierarchy (what contains what); relationships back to users — there are eleven of them, and nearly every table has one — are written inline inside each box in rust instead of drawn as eleven more lines crossing the whole diagram.
